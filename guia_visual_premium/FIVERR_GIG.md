## 📝 Descripción del gig

```
Your AI-generated character keeps changing face every time you regenerate it? I fix that.

I work with Midjourney's advanced reference system (character reference, style
reference, fixed seeds, controlled character weight) to lock your character's
face, outfit, and style across dozens of images — so your brand, comic, game,
or content series always looks like it came from the same artist.

What you get:
✅ A fixed "canon sheet" of your character (front / side / three-quarter)
✅ Multiple consistent poses and expressions built from that canon
✅ One unified visual style across every image — no random palette or lighting shifts
✅ High-resolution files ready for social media, print, or product use

Perfect for: brand mascots, book/comic characters, game assets, streaming
avatars, and social content series.

I don't just generate pretty images — I build a repeatable system, so future
images (yours or mine) keep matching this character indefinitely.

Message me before ordering if your project needs something outside the
packages below — happy to customize.
```

## 📋 Requisitos al comprador (campo "Requirements" de Fiverr)

```
1. Describe your character (age, key features) or attach a reference photo/sketch.
2. Pick one style: Pixar 3D / Hyperrealistic / Watercolor / Cyberpunk Neon /
   Minimalist — or say "surprise me" and I'll suggest one based on your project.
3. Where will this character be used? (brand, book, game, social media...)
   This changes which poses I prioritize.
4. Any brand colors or preferences? (HEX codes if you have them.)
5. Confirm the format you need: square, vertical, or both.
```

## ❓ FAQ del gig

**Which AI tool do you use?**
Midjourney, using its character-reference and style-reference systems plus fixed seeds — this is what makes the consistency possible.

**Can you match a character I already generated somewhere else?**
Often yes — send me your best existing image as a reference and I'll evaluate it before you order.

**Do I own the rights to the images?**
Yes, full commercial usage rights transfer to you on delivery.

**What if the character doesn't look right after delivery?**
Revisions are included per package above — I refine based on your feedback within that scope.

**Can you do more than 20 poses?**
Yes — add extra poses as a gig extra, or message me for a custom quote on larger batches (e.g. a full 100-image content month, as covered in the Scaling chapter of my own workflow).

## 🖼️ Imagen de portada del gig

Monta un **grid antes/después**: a la izquierda, 4 imágenes de "el mismo personaje" con la cara cambiando (el error típico); a la derecha, tu hoja canon real con 4 poses idénticas de rasgos. Usa la paleta `#0A192F` / `#FF6B35` del manual de estilo como firma visual — así el gig ya transmite "sistema", no "until ves qué sale".

> ### ⚠️ Antes de publicar
> Fiverr penaliza mucho los gigs sin muestras de portafolio. Genera **2 personajes de ejemplo completos** (siguiendo el Capítulo 4 de la guía) antes de publicar, y súbelos como imágenes de galería del gig — sin eso, el algoritmo casi no te muestra a compradores nuevos.
